## Concepto con montura (paso 1)
New, different character. Create ONE full-body character concept art illustration in exactly the same art style, rendering, line quality, level of costume detail, color saturation and mood as the three reference images in my first message of this chat. Do not reuse colors or design from the previous characters.

Character: Riona, young woman around 20, auburn hair in a long braid wrapped with bronze rings, warm sun-touched skin, sharp amber eyes. The last squire of a fading draconic order that guards a temple hill. Concept: she wears the order like a promise she is the only one left to keep. Costume: layered ritual robes and a lamellar scale coat in ember orange and bone white, with dark crimson underlayers, gold trim and dragon-scale motifs embroidered along the borders, a high collar, a short bone-white cape with a gold sun disc clasp, leather riding gloves and tall boots. Mix Byzantine, Middle Eastern and East Asian influences, no obvious ancient Greek costume. Aetherpunk details in polished brass with a small glowing amber aether crystal set in her pauldron. Weapon: a long lance with a flame-shaped blade and red tassels.
She stands on the ground beside her griffin mount, one hand resting on its neck. The griffin has golden-brown feathers, a white eagle head, lion hindquarters, folded wings and a brass and leather saddle with ember-orange saddlecloth. Full figures of both, nothing cut, solid light background, no scenery.
