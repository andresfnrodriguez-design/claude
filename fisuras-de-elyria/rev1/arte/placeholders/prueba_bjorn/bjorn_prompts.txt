Character: Bjorn, young man around 20, from Kaldholt, a cold northern nation. Moss-green hair cut short at the sides with a longer top tied in a small knot, pale skin with windburned cheeks, a faint scar through one eyebrow, broad shoulders, open honest face. Frontier cavalry who grew up raising elk on the border. Concept: a lancer who would rather be herding than fighting, the warmest heart in a cold army. Costume: a long quilted riding coat in deep forest green and ivory, split into tabard panels with silver and gold knotwork embroidery, a thick grey wolf-fur mantle, iron scale pauldrons and bracers with brass fittings, braided leather straps, a wide belt with a horn-shaped brass aether lantern glowing amber, fur-lined cold-weather boots. Mix medieval northern, Byzantine and East Asian steppe influences, no obvious ancient Greek costume. Weapon: a long cavalry lance with a pennant.
He stands on the ground beside his mount, a huge northern elk with broad antlers wrapped in small brass bells and ribbons, a green and ivory saddle blanket and a leather saddle. Full figures of both, nothing cut, solid light background, no scenery.
